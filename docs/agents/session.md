## Gotchas & Uncommitted Discoveries
- Inlining question stems and choices directly into Alpine attributes causes massive HTML escaping inflation (`&quot;`), producing ~1.84MB documents. Moving data payloads into `<script type="application/json">` tags avoids entity inflation and parsing overhead.
- Keeping `div#panel-analysis[role="tabpanel"]` in the DOM while wrapping its internal children in `<template x-if="analysisMounted">` satisfies WCAG tabpanel relationships without evaluating Alpine sub-trees or instantiating 8,000+ DOM nodes on other tabs.
- `TeacherClassManagementTest::test_student_can_open_assignments_scoped_to_one_class` verifies that when `?classroom=...` is present on the query string, other classes' assignments are not rendered in the server response. Local dropdown switching must perform a navigation/refetch rather than client-only `replaceState` to maintain backend isolation.
